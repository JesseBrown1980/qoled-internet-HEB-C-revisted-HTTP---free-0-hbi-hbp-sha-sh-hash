HOLD H7 | file=qutrit/README.md | severity=medium
OLD<<<
**Interference in the state and the windows.** The 16-qutrit state runs 40 cycles of interfering layers. A window is a chain, so a three-entry transfer vector gives the same amplitudes in m steps.
>>>
NEW<<<
**Interference in the state and the windows.** The 16-qutrit state runs 40 cycles of interfering layers. A window is a chain, so a three-entry transfer vector gives the same amplitudes in m steps. A window's amplitude row is A(y) = 3⁻ᵐ Σ_w ζ^(local(w) + 3w·y), conditioned on the other qutrits' branch. In the register that amplitude is 3^(−(n−m)/2) · ζ^rest_phase · A(y), with rest_phase in the same receipt row. A(y) is a product of 3×3 unitaries, so 3ᵐp is at most 3 at every m, which is why the fraction above 3 reads 0.
>>>
REASON: The rows are conditional and carry no rest_phase, stated nowhere; verified by counts over all 3^m terms and full recomputation at m=3..16 (out/own_window.hbp, out/window_labels.hbp).
